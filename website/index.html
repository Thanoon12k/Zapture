GOOD QUESTIONS

A few things
worth knowing.

Explore the docs
Is Zapture really free?

Yes. Zapture is open-source software under the MIT license. You can use, share, and modify it under that license. No subscription or account is needed.

Where do my recordings go?

Windows defaults to your Videos/Zapture folder, with separate Recordings and Screenshots folders. Choose another location in Settings and turn automatic folder opening on or off for each capture type.

Does anything get uploaded?

Zapture saves captures locally and has no built-in cloud upload or telemetry. If you connect an AI client through MCP, that client may process screenshots according to its own settings and policies.

Which platforms are ready?

Windows is the primary release. Linux X11 and macOS builds are experimental and require FFmpeg. Wayland capture and mobile apps are not implemented. On macOS, computer audio needs a loopback device and manual configuration. See the documentation for prerequisites.

Can I use it with my AI assistant?

Yes, if your assistant supports custom local stdio MCP servers and the 2025-06-18 protocol. Zapture provides screenshot, start, stop, and status tools. It does not provide mouse, keyboard, or arbitrary shell control.

How do I update or report a problem?

Exit Zapture, extract the new release, and launch it. Existing settings are preserved. Report an issue on GitHub with your OS, version, and steps to reproduce it.
