Privacy, simply.

This website

This website uses our self-hosted visitor-dashboard to understand how people discover and use Zapture. It records page visits, timestamps, IP addresses, referrers, browser and device details, language, screen dimensions, approximate country and internet provider, time on page, scroll depth, and clicks on links and buttons. It does not record your screen, microphone, or form-field contents.

A first-party cookie named vd_id helps recognise returning browsers and can last up to two years. Visits are retained for up to 180 days. Dashboard login and owner-exclusion cookies are used only when you log in to the private dashboard. You can remove cookies through your browser settings; without a cookie the package may group visits using the IP address and browser user-agent.

Analytics are stored in a private database on this PythonAnywhere account and are not publicly accessible. Approximate country/provider information is looked up locally using DB-IP Lite data, licensed under CC BY 4.0. Online IP lookup and Telegram notifications are disabled. Country information may be inaccurate for VPNs or mobile networks; city-level data is not configured.

PythonAnywhere also manages technical hosting logs. Fonts load from Google Fonts, which receives the request metadata needed to serve them. Downloads and documentation link to GitHub, whose own privacy terms apply. The interactive recorder on the landing page is only a simulation.

The desktop app

Zapture saves screenshots and recordings on your computer. It does not include telemetry, accounts, or automatic cloud upload. You choose what to capture and where to save it.

AI and MCP

MCP is optional. When you connect a local MCP client, that client can request captures. Your chosen AI client may send images to its own service. Review that client's settings and privacy policy before connecting it.

Questions

You can open an issue on GitHub. Do not include private recordings, credentials, or sensitive personal information in public issues.
